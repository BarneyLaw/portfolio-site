---

Setup was effortless, and that is not faint praise. Installing the client on a
laptop, a phone, and three cluster nodes took about ten minutes total, and NAT
traversal worked from networks where I fully expected to end up relayed through
DERP. Subnet routing and MagicDNS meant I could reach internal services by name
without touching the router's firewall at all.

The friction is the account model. The free tier's device and user limits are
generous right up until you start adding VMs, and ACL changes live in a hosted
control plane I do not run. For a personal network that is a reasonable trade;
for the thing that gets me back into the homelab when something is broken, I'd
rather the control plane be mine.

So: mixed. It works exactly as advertised, and I'm migrating to Headscale
anyway. If you don't share that itch, the hosted version is the better use of
your evening.
