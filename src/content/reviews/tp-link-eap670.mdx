Fine specs on paper: Wi-Fi 6, 2.5G uplink, PoE+, a sensible mounting bracket.
On a spec sheet comparison against anything in its price bracket it wins or
ties.

The Omada controller is where it fell apart. Adopting the AP required the
controller to be reachable at exactly the right moment, and a failed adoption
left the device in a state that only a paperclip reset would clear. Config
changes routinely took two attempts to apply, and the software controller wanted
more of a VM than an access point's management plane has any business needing.

The hardware also ran hot enough to be uncomfortable to hold after an hour of
moderate traffic, in a ceiling mount with nothing insulating it. Whether that
shortens its life I never found out.

Returned it after a week. The radios were probably fine; I couldn't get far
enough past the management layer to care.
